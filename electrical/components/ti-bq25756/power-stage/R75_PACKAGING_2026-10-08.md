# R75 packaging-only sourcing update

8 October 2026. No resistance, tolerance, rating, footprint or circuit change.

Vishay WSL document 30100, revision 23 November 2023, page 1 note 2 explicitly
identifies EB as the lead-free 1000-piece reel packaging equivalent of EA;
only reel quantity differs. Page 3 lists the WSL2512 EA reel as 2000 pieces.
Therefore `WSL25125L000FEB` has the same WSL2512 / 5 mOhm / 1% / 1 W electrical
core and package as the previously selected `WSL25125L000FEA`. It is not WSLP,
not the 5% J tolerance option, and not a high-power upgrade.
